Crisis Support

You are not alone.
Help is available right now.

Whether you are in crisis, supporting someone in crisis, or trying to help a vulnerable person — these resources provide immediate, confidential support.

💔 Suicide Prevention

If you are thinking about ending your life, or someone you know is — please reach out now. Trained counsellors are available 24/7 to listen without judgment.

🏠 Domestic Violence

If you are experiencing violence, abuse, or control at home — you deserve safety. Confidential help, shelter referrals, and legal support are available immediately.

🛡️ Child Safety

If a child is being abused, exploited, or is in danger — report it now. Every call could save a child's life. Calls are confidential and free.

Stay Safe

If you are in danger,
these steps can help.

📍
Know Your Location
Always know your full address so you can communicate it clearly to emergency services.
📱
Save Emergency Numbers
Save local emergency numbers and one trusted person's number in your phone right now.
🤝
Tell Someone You Trust
Share your situation with at least one trusted person — a friend, relative, teacher, or neighbour.
