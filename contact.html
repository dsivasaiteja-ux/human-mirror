Before You Write

Quick answers to
common questions.

Is Human Mirror free to use? +

Yes. Human Mirror is completely free for every user, everywhere in the world. There are no fees, no subscriptions, and no premium tiers. It is a global public service.

Can Human Mirror help me find a specific resource in my country? +

Yes — use the search on our homepage and describe your situation. The AI will find the best local resources. If we don't have coverage in your area yet, contact us and we'll do our best to help directly.

How can I report an incorrect or outdated resource? +

Please use the contact form above and select "Report an Incorrect Resource." Include the resource name, what's wrong, and (if possible) the correct information. We'll review and update it within 48 hours.

I want to add a resource for my country. How? +

Wonderful! Select "Suggest a New Resource" in the contact form, or apply to become a Resource Curator on our Volunteer page. Curators can add and verify resources directly.

Is my message to Human Mirror confidential? +

Yes. Your message is only seen by Human Mirror team members working to respond to your enquiry. We never share, sell, or publish contact messages. Your email address is only used to reply to you.
