Government Agency Partners

Official government listing with verified badge
API integration for real-time scheme information
Multilingual resource pages in regional languages
Priority placement in location-based search results
Joint public awareness campaigns

Custom Human Mirror portal for your institution
Student mental health and wellbeing resources
Career guidance and scholarship directory integration
Research collaboration opportunities
Student volunteer programme co-certification
For Schools & Universities

Corporate Partners

Purpose-driven companies can support Human Mirror's mission through ethical corporate partnerships — funding platform development, sponsoring resource curation in specific regions, supporting employee volunteering, or integrating Human Mirror into their employee wellbeing programmes.

Named sponsorship of specific global regions or categories
Employee volunteer programme through Human Mirror
ESG and CSR impact reporting and certification
Co-branded awareness campaigns
Ethical partnership — no advertising or data access
For Companies & Corporates

Partnership Process

How to become
a partner.

Our partnership process is simple, transparent, and built around mission alignment. We do not accept partners whose values conflict with human dignity, privacy, or inclusion.

1
Submit an Enquiry
Fill in the partnership inquiry form with details about your organisation, your services, and the type of partnership you are seeking.
2
Mission Alignment Review
Our team reviews your organisation to ensure alignment with Human Mirror's values of humanity, trust, privacy, and inclusion — typically within 5 business days.
3
Partnership Call
We schedule a call to discuss the specific partnership structure, what you offer, and how Human Mirror can best connect people to your services.
4
Go Live
Your services are listed, verified, and activated in the Human Mirror platform — with AI-powered referrals starting immediately.
🤝
Enquiry Received!
Thank you for your interest in partnering with Human Mirror. Our team will review your enquiry and respond within 5 business days.
